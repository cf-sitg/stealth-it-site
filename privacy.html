Privacy Notice

Last updated: May 2, 2026

Stealth IT Group keeps initial contact simple. We do not use a general website contact form, and we do not collect unnecessary information through this website.

Information we collect

If you schedule an introductory call through our Google Calendar booking page, we may receive information you provide during booking, such as:

  • Name
  • Business email address
  • Company name
  • Meeting details you choose to provide

What not to submit

Please do not include passwords, customer records, regulated data, sensitive logs, production credentials, confidential documents, or other sensitive information in your booking request.

How we use information

We use the information you provide only to respond to your inquiry, schedule and manage the conversation, understand the general nature of your request, and follow up on services you asked about.

Lawful basis

Where privacy laws such as the GDPR apply, we process this information based on your request to engage with us, our legitimate interest in responding to business inquiries, and, where applicable, steps taken before entering into a services relationship.

Where information is processed

Scheduling information is handled through Google Workspace / Google Calendar. We may also retain related business communications in our Google Workspace account.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and scheduling information only as long as needed to manage the conversation, maintain reasonable business records, and meet legal or administrative needs. If no services relationship follows, we will delete or minimize the information when it is no longer needed.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not add booking contacts to a marketing list. We only share information where needed to provide services, use our business systems, comply with law, or protect our rights.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to certain processing of your personal information.

Contact

To make a privacy request, use the same business contact channel you used to schedule with us or raise it during your scheduled conversation.
